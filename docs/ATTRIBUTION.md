# Attribution and terms

## Original project

CutOut is Elad Richardson's Android scribble-segmentation project. The original [summary](summary.pdf) and [poster](poster.pdf) credit Anastasia Dubrovina and Aaron Wetzler as supervisors. The recovered application's About text credits Elad Richardson, Anastasia Dubrovina and Ron Kimmel, lists Aaron Wetzler for additional technical supervision, and credits Michal Hendel's help with interface and logo design. The original wording remains in the preserved resources.

Elad supplied the [Android archive](../source/original/android-app/) and authorized hosting recovered project code. This records his permission only; it does not speak for other contributors, replace third-party terms, establish authorship of the separate prototype or authorize a new manuscript. The [source guide](original-android.md) maps the original operators and build records.

The three-file [related MATLAB/C++ prototype](../source/original/related-prototype/) is connected to the project through matching archived outputs, but its authorship is unverified. Its missing generic heap was not redistributed because its attribution and terms could not be established.

## Updated implementation

The native port, fixes, platform wrappers and drawing interface are separately labeled updated work written in 2026 by Codex for Aaron Wetzler. Recovered algorithms remain attributed to Elad and their original module authors. [Native provenance](../updated/native/PROVENANCE.json) maps copied source hashes and intentional changes.

The separate Python exercise derives its local numerical formula from the related prototype. Its queue, boundary handling, multiple-source support, renderer, notebook, tests and procedural label-composition example are updated work; they are not recovered Android behavior. See its [guide](../updated/README.md) and [source manifest](../source/PROVENANCE.json).

## Dependencies and figures

Original ANN, FIGTree, MegaWave2/FLST and Java notices remain in their files. The [original-source terms](original-android.md#attribution-and-terms) identify their authors and scope; [supplementary license references](licenses/README.md) provide retrieved license texts and hashes. OpenCV and platform dependencies retain their own terms. No blanket repository license is assigned to this mixed archive or derived work.

The historical interface figure is preserved project imagery. The desktop screenshot comes from an actual updated run. Its coffee photograph is by Rachel Michetti, courtesy of Pikolo Espresso Bar, distributed under CC0 through scikit-image; the [fixture guide](../updated/validation/fixtures/README.md) records the pinned source, hash and new demonstration scribbles.

Authors may open an issue to correct attribution, contribute updates or request removal.
