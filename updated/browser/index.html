CutOut

Extract an object from foreground and background scribbles. The shared C++ segmentation core runs locally as WebAssembly; your image is not uploaded.

Choose an image or load the example. Mark the object in green and the background in red.

Image and scribbles

Click or drag to draw. Later strokes replace earlier labels. Images are resized to at most 1,000 pixels on their longest side; downloads use that size. Transparent inputs are composited on white.
